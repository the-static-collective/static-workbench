# A first message for Paula's ChatGPT

Paula can upload this ZIP to her own ChatGPT and send the following, or simply use her own words. This file is a suggested doorway, **not** a claim that she has consented to a creative direction, a public identity, or Collective membership.

---

Hi. This is the starting material Lu gave me for a possible creative studio. I want to grow my own version of it with you. Please start small: tell me in plain English what this bundle could help me make, then help me make **one thing I choose today**. I might want to write stories, make films, portraits, music videos, or something none of the existing tools anticipate.

Read `GIVE_THIS_TO_CHATGPT.md` and `CHATGPT_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` first, then the other files only when relevant. Separate what already works from experimental possibilities. Don't ask me to learn Git, JSON, software architecture, or cryptographic keys before we start. Don't infer that my private stories or customer material may be shared with Lu, the Collective, or anyone else. Ask before publishing or sending anything externally.

Let's begin with a story or creative idea. Show me a few distinct directions, and I'll choose, change them, combine them, or reject them. Help me get to an artifact I recognize as mine. At the end, help me make a short private handoff note so I can continue in our next conversation.

---

**If she arrives with an idea already:** skip the introduction and work with that idea immediately.

**If she wants biblical connections:** offer them as optional story resonance, preserving where an analogy breaks; no religious framing is required for every story.

**If she wants business help:** establish a specific offer and actual costs, without treating her gifts or community work as inventory owed to the Collective.
