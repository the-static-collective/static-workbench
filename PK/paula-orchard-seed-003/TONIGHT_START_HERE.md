# TONIGHT — One real story. One independently repeatable doorway.

**For Lu and Paula. This is an optional session overlay on the existing Paula Story Workbench.** Open `tonight/SESSION_BOARD.html` locally for a simple live board, or follow this page without the board. Paula can start with ChatGPT alone. Do not require Git, a local Node installation, keys, or the entire Collective to begin.

## Actual aim

Before the call ends, Paula has (a) one story direction she chose, (b) a board or first playable short, (c) her own ChatGPT doorway and the ability to return without Lu, and (d) one clearly stated next creative action. If filming/generation is not ready, one approved kernel plus a usable board and copy-ready shot prompts counts as a meaningful first landing. A keypair, public registry, paid sale, or actual finished film is **not** required.

## 0–10 minutes — Arrive, don't lecture

1. Send `TONIGHT_PAULA_OPEN_FIRST.md` or share this zip. Her ChatGPT should begin in normal conversation.
2. Ask: **What do you want to make?** Fiction, film, portrait, food story, music video, or something else are all legitimate. The prebuilt short-film workflow is a starter, not a limit.
3. Paula chooses what is recorded or shared. If this is a call between you, explicitly ask before recording her speech, uploading personal/customer material, using her face or voice, or creating a public draft.

## 10–35 minutes — Produce a creative decision

1. Take one raw thought directly to Paula's ChatGPT (`STORYDROP`) or the existing Story Desk.
2. Let ChatGPT show 1–3 story kernels. **Paula chooses KEEP/HOLD/REVISE.** A model suggestion is not a creative approval.
3. Capture the minimum: her own working title, desired feeling, one recognizable image, and what she wants the audience to experience.
4. Make a 20–60-second short board *only if that fits her goal*. A still-image portrait, narrated story, music fragment, or treatment is equally valid.

## 35–75 minutes — Make a visible thing

1. Create one hero reference, storyboard image, short clip, narrated scene, or visual treatment; use her available creative tool and actual credits, not a presumed vendor-specific feature.
2. If video: get a **HERO** frame/shot, a **BRIDGE** if movement matters, and **TEXTURE** material only if needed. Save the selected material separately from rejected experiments.
3. Ask her which result feels like hers, rather than escalating generation count automatically.
4. If an experiment blocks the result, fall back to stills + narration + a simple edit, or end with the board and prompts.

## 75–105 minutes — Build only at the point of friction

Lu can run `TONIGHT_BUILD_MISSIONS.md` in a separate builder environment. The missions are staged. Do not replace the existing Story Desk just because another UI looks more impressive. The first actual friction determines which build mission receives credits.

## 105–120 minutes — Transfer the keys to the *workflow*, not a private key

1. Paula opens her own conversation and can tell it what she wants next without calling Lu.
2. Save her approved continuation and any deliverables privately. Share exact files only with her consent. Keep customer names and other people's stories private by default.
3. Complete `TONIGHT_HANDOFF.md` together. Record owner, next action, asset location (not secrets), permission to share, actual time/cost, and one optional experiment.
4. **Do not create her world identity, sign for her, declare membership, publish, or commit raw source material on her behalf.** Worldbody, keyring, and Chronomark remain available as future doors.

## Supported floor vs experimental frontier

- Existing: Story Workbench supports conversational intake, kept kernels, structured story cards and Flow-ready shot batches; the Orchard/Worldbody packages contain bounded local test specimens. Inspect `README.md`, `GIVE_THIS_TO_CHATGPT.md`, and `WORLDBODY_001.md` for their actual scope.
- Proposed: fully composed live donor runtimes, independently authenticated inter-world handoff, robust audiovisual watermarking, automatic public world census, and external platform publishing are **not present** in this bundle.

## Open at most two surfaces during the call

**Paula:** her ChatGPT (+ her creative tool once she wants to generate). **Lu:** `tonight/SESSION_BOARD.html` or the existing Story Desk. The full orchard stays in the package, not on Paula's screen.
