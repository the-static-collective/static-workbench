## Canon candidate 001 — The handoff carries capacity, not control

An Orchard Seed is a portable creative starting point another person may independently receive, revise, refuse, abandon, and grow with their own ChatGPT and tools. The handoff is successful when the recipient makes something not prescribed by the giver. Neither ancestry nor a signed offer grants the giver continuing authority over the recipient's world.

**Human door:** Begin with the recipient's actual creative intent. Do not require an infrastructure tutorial to make a first film or other artifact.

## The seven independently useful seeds

1. **Orchard Seed / Human Door:** portable studio, creative continuity, self-directed growth.
2. **Ethical Kernel / Membrane:** proposals, capability availability, verified approvals, and executable authority remain distinct; protected actions require a relevant authorization boundary.
3. **Worldbody / Genealogy:** local world identities and typed, evidenced relations; a world, repository, member, and fictional subworld are distinct kinds of entity.
4. **Jubilee / Historical Embodiment:** reproducible projections of bounded, attributable acts and relations; preserve particulars and unresolved evidence rather than scoring people or claiming global consensus.
5. **Ignition / Keyring:** independent world cryptographic identities, signed offers, separate recipient acceptance, local key lifecycles, and source-verifiable artifact manifests.
6. **Fractal Signature × Chronomark:** experimental media carrier for recovering a *candidate* artifact/world/segment reference after media transformation; a detected watermark never substitutes for provenance verification.
7. **Creative-economic return:** the system must help the recipient complete meaningful creative work, sustain customer relationships, and assess actual costs. Genealogy does not create royalty claims or ownership.

## Existing organ boundaries and the proposed composition

- **Founder Node:** discover evidenced nearby doors; discovery does not authorize entry.
- **TranchNode:** retain typed ancestry, changes, unresolved disputes, and staged continuity; similarity is not descent.
- **Corpus OS:** evaluate declared actor/capacity/power/operation, warrant use, consequence, refusal, and causal anomalies. Computational receipts do not authenticate external people or confer legal status by themselves.
- **Jubilee Engine VM:** compile reproducible, bounded historical graph/act representations; a verifier confirms the supplied data and process reproduce the claimed result, not that every source claim is true.
- **LOADOUT / Project 0 / Human Witness / MEMENTO:** complementary capabilities for bounded composition, shared meaning, encountered human acts, and admission of fictional canon. Their individual authority boundaries should remain intact.

The prior local Orchard and Worldbody specimens provide a starting point. Full cryptographic inter-world acceptance, network discovery, audiovisual watermark robustness, and live donor-runtime composition are **unbuilt or unproven by this conversation**.

## Three non-collapses

1. `signed bytes != verified human intent != receiving-world authorization`
2. `detected watermark != authentic artifact != a true historical account`
3. `registered local world != verified member != global census != publicly discoverable person`

Further: `seed offered != seed accepted`; `ancestor != owner`; `derived from != inspired by`; `publication intent != platform-observed publication`; `world-time != artifact-time != publication-time`; `absence of a record != refusal`; `act receipt != human worth or economic value`.

## Ignition protocol — proposed

1. A recipient chooses to establish a world; nothing is registered solely because the ZIP is opened.
2. Their trusted environment generates a fresh signing identity with secure randomness; the private key does not derive from public seed bytes, Mandelbrot parameters, a public clock, or a phrase.
3. A versioned world declaration records its public-key fingerprint, exact seed digest, origin claim, disclosure settings, and independent local authority boundary.
4. The origin signs an exact **offer**. The recipient inspects the package and separately signs or otherwise independently authorizes an exact **acceptance**. Authentication of who actually controls the signing key requires out-of-band or separately governed evidence.
5. A world genealogical relation is admitted only with both sides' applicable evidence; disputed, refused, missing, or private relations remain distinguishable.
6. Derive public watermark pattern parameters from public world/artifact identifiers through a versioned deterministic process; never use the world signing key as a watermark secret.
7. Keys may be rotated, retired, compromised, or lost; retain signed historical references without silently assigning old-key authority to a new key. Private signing keys remain outside chat transcripts, Git repos, and exported seeds.

## Chronomark — three clocks and a fourth relationship

- **Artifact clock:** sample/frame rate, rational media timestamp, musical meter or beat map, and a declared timebase. A segment may have its own local timeline.
- **World clock:** attributable recorded occurrences and causal order; an ordinary signed timestamp alone is not independent proof of wall-clock time.
- **Publication clock:** intended phase/time versus observed service/platform time and receipt; platforms may queue or truncate timestamps.
- **Timewarp map:** if work B remixes work A, store an optional *piecewise source-time → descendant-time* mapping, including trimmed/looped/reversed or speed-altered regions, with explicit confidence and provenance. A derivative may map several intervals to one destination or leave gaps. It must not claim perfect reconstruction where source segments are missing.

The 0–9 preferred-second phase is a voluntary aesthetic/publishing ritual (e.g., seconds whose ones digit is 7), **not** authentication. Phase has only ten possibilities and cannot uniquely identify an artwork or prove authorship. Never change reported publication timestamps to force the phase.

## Fractal Signature — proposed, not validated

World-ID/public-key fingerprint and artifact ID identify a versioned, reproducible *candidate* pattern. Actual survivability requires a tested embedding/detection method using signal-processing tools, synchronization, redundant encoding, and threshold calibration. Mandelbrot self-similarity alone does not confer crop/resize/JPEG/rotation resistance. Embed a compact manifest locator rather than an entire public key when capacity is constrained; retain signed binding evidence in the manifest. A copied watermark can occur in a forged artifact: detection only locates a candidate record.

Use conventional robust watermarking as a baseline; collect detection rate, false-positive rate, distortion, payload, and recovery under rescale, crop, trim, speed change, transcoding, screenshots, and unrelated-media attacks. If the fractal element does not improve measurable behavior, keep it as optional artwork rather than security-critical infrastructure.

## Fresh fruit from recomposition

**TIMEWARP GENEALOGY:** An excerpt or remix can point to a specific source interval through an explicit transformation map. This is a stronger descriptive relation than matching 0–9 temporal phases and remains useful even if the watermark is lost.

**THE KEYED DOOR:** A creative world or ARG can embed a signed invitation in an artifact. The signed invitation proves an attributable exact offer; the receiving world still determines present access. It may survive loss of the original website when the relevant evidence is retained.

**THE SILENT ORCHARD:** An independent world may stop communicating or withhold genealogy. Its absence from a public map cannot be used as proof that it ceased to exist, rejected membership, or performed no acts. Any public count is a count of voluntary, evidenced declarations within a stated scope and cut.

**THE WORLD'S AUTOBIOGRAPHY:** A world can render an authorized retrospective from local receipts: not one official narrative, but a traceable story of which offers, works, partnerships, failed experiments, and divergent creative choices it chooses to disclose. Unshared particulars stay private.

**THE ARTIFACT AS DOOR:** A recovered media mark or sidecar reference could help a future recipient find an archived, human-readable seed/manifest and independently inspect its ancestry. Recoverability requires persistent storage and locators; a signature cannot guarantee long-term availability.

**RELATIONSHIP WORLDS:** Two independently governed orchards may constitute a bounded collaboration with its own identity, source history, approvals, and dissolution record. It does not become a parent or owner of either participant.

**THE FRUITLESS FRUIT:** A refused collaboration, unmade film, broken adapter, or unsuccessful commission can leave a bounded experiment receipt and a future design question without being promoted into success, fictional canon, a reputation penalty, or private surveillance.

## First executable crossing: KWB × Chronomark 001

Use two simulated offline worlds and disposable test keys. Sign an exact origin seed offer; verify it in a separate recipient environment; record an independent acceptance; create a signed test-artifact manifest; embed a short candidate locator and time-variant mark in a disposable video/audio specimen; transform it; attempt recovery and manifest verification. Keep genealogical admission separate from watermark detection and from simulated authorization.

**Adversarial tests:** changed seed bytes; forged and copied signatures; unknown key; key retirement/compromise; recipient refusal; self-asserted ancestry; unauthorized parent control; private relation leakage; missing parent evidence; repeated or reordered receipts; cropped and re-encoded imagery; trimmed or speed-changed audio/video; copied watermark; phase collisions; observed publishing time differing from requested time.

**Success means:** the recipient can independently accept and grow; bounded shared evidence remains verifiable; unauthorized authority does not cross; candidates are recoverable with measured error rates; the creator can still complete a film when the experimental pathway fails.
