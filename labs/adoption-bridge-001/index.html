STATIC COLLECTIVE / PK LAB 001

Orchard Bridge

A small workbench for discovering existing adoption-support organizations and testing a human-authorized referral handoff—without moving private case records into a shared directory.

EXPERIMENT · NO LIVE REFERRALS
Public information + fictional encounters only. Do not enter names, addresses, adoption records, case information, or any other personal data. This standalone page has no account, backend, messaging, live referral transmission, identity check, or legal-consent verification. The organizations below are public directory leads, not confirmed partners or a statement of service availability.
PUBLIC DISCOVERY

Who could help with what?

Search documented capabilities. An appearance in the atlas is not participation in this project. Confirm current service rules directly with the listed organization.

THE CONNECTION

Capabilities, not case files

A public directory can show that an organization provides a particular service. It does not show that the organization can accept a particular referral today.

Discovery → inquiry → authorized handoff → confirmation.
Each crossing requires its own verification. The atlas never triggers a referral or creates an account with any listed organization.

First research questions

  1. 1Which organizations have accurate public service descriptions and referral instructions?
  2. 2Where do adult adoptees and families repeatedly need to navigate separate service directories?
  3. 3What is the smallest handoff two willing organizations can jointly validate?

    INVARIANTS

    What this build refuses

    1. ×It does not match children with adults, approve placements, or assess adoption eligibility.
    2. ×It does not share case records, transfer consent, or contact an organization.
    3. ×It does not infer that a signature proves identity, capacity, or comprehension.
    4. ×It does not equate referral sent, accepted, reported, and confirmed.
    5. ×It does not treat revocation as retroactively erasing an earlier occurrence or already-disclosed data.
