# GRAFT 002 — Native candidate table and Dogram linkage

Status: experimental, deterministic candidate questions added to existing HOUSE
native Maxhinal rides. See [Structural Witness 001](graft-dogram-structural-witness-001.md)
for the distinct calculation boundary.

## Use

Open a saved HOUSE native Maxhinal ride. In the GRAFT section, declare
**KEEP** (human intention to preserve), **BEND** (human-selected flexible aspect),
**INTRUDER** (human-selected foreign mechanism), an exploratory relation lane,
and one FORK!-inspired move: fuse, invert, continue, or wildcard.

**Preview three GRAFT candidate questions**, review the exact immutable round,
and save it explicitly. The direct, side-door and productive-wrong-turn cards
are deterministic *questions* and suggested small experiments, not a model's
interpretation of your selected materials. They are not three ready-to-use
implementations; no unselected file is read, no project is modified, no
participation is requested, and no candidate is automatically harvested.

A saved round has its own SHA-256 content identity, carries its parent's saved
ride and source refs, and preserves all three candidates even when one is
selected. Reopen earlier rounds from the ride. **Select this proposal for
Dogram** only when you intend to measure a separately reviewed hypothetical
structure for that particular candidate.

The subsequent Dogram graph form remains *human declared*. Its preview pins
the chosen candidate's content address into the Dogram specimen metadata,
along with the parent ride digest. Changing candidate selection between
preview and measure refuses the old frozen specimen. Existing ride-only
witnesses remain valid with no candidate selected.

## Remaining frontiers

The candidate table does not yet compose completed song drafts, executable
features, an automatic graph, seedFORK semantic retrieval, or BananaGram
community requests. "Select" means only a local candidate-specific
**measurement association**, not an overall judgment, final disposition,
evidence of feasibility, publication, or an actual community commitment.
A later full GRAFT chamber can use these immutable seeds and Dogram
calculation receipts as separate inputs to a genuinely human-approved act.
