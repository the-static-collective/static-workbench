THE FIRST DOOR

The house is open.

Make one Seed. Compose two Seeds into a Machine. Give the Machine a third Seed and a rule to sketch a World. Bring your work back through another door.

This is an optional, openly fictional, single-user local game. Every object is a Workbench-owned creative sketch, not a project-native receipt, a real execution capability, or a claim about the world.

Reading the local House…

Entering creates a local persistence record on this machine. You can return to ordinary Workbench at any time.

01 / MAKE

Plant a Seed

A source of your own: a line, mechanic, story premise, or creative idea. Nothing is imported or published automatically.

02 / COMPOSE

Build a Machine

Choose two distinct Seeds. The Machine preserves both exact source digests and their composed prompt. It does not execute instructions.

03 / INHABIT

Sketch a World

Combine one Machine with a fresh Seed not used by that Machine. Declare one actual rule for playing in your new fictional World.

04 / REDISCOVER

Your local artifacts and doors

Each creation is an immutable artifact. Composing something new never overwrites its inputs. Choose a doorway on a World to leave an encounter trace and visit another local Workbench instrument.

05 / RETURN

Doorway journal
